## Cookies

Cookies are little piece of information that is stored in the browser.
Everytime the user visits this site the data will be sent to the server.
Like a trail of crumbs about youself left in the website you visited.

Common uses:
- Session management
- Personalization
- Tracking 

Signing a cookie - you can verify the integrity of the cookie, if the content is the same, its not hiding the data
